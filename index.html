API keys

Keys are stored only in this browser (localStorage) and sent only to the provider they belong to. All three have free tiers.

  • Twelve Data: twelvedata.com/pricing (Basic, free): daily bars, 800 calls/day.
  • Alpha Vantage: alphavantage.co/support/#api-key: the free tier gives the last 100 daily bars only (too short for the 200-day average or a 12-month backtest).
  • Finnhub: finnhub.io/register: company news on the free tier.
  • No key? Export daily OHLCV as CSV from your broker or a charting site and use "Upload CSV".
  • No key needed: "Daily watchlist" reads prices a scheduled GitHub Action downloads every weekday for the tickers in data/watchlist.json (about 30 US and 37 Indian stocks). Edit that file on GitHub to change the list.
  • India: Twelve Data is asked for NSE/BSE directly, but its free plan may not include Indian exchanges; the app shows Twelve Data's own message if not. Alpha Vantage's free key covers BSE (last 100 days). For full history, download a CSV from nseindia.com (Get Quote → Historical Data) or bseindia.com, or export from your broker (e.g. Zerodha Console), then use "Upload CSV". Free news covers US companies only.
